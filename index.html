Tonys GTA Clipper

A personal desktop tool that edits GTA 6 gameplay clips (captions, hook text, vertical 9:16 format, creator credit) and sends them to the creator's own TikTok account as drafts. The creator reviews and publishes every draft in the TikTok app.

Clips are posted on TikTok and YouTube.
