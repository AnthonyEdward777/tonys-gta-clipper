Tonys GTA Clipper - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Tonys GTA Clipper is a personal desktop tool that edits GTA 6 gameplay clips and sends them to the user's own TikTok account as drafts. It is run by one person for their own TikTok account.

What we access
When you connect TikTok, the app receives only what TikTok grants for the permissions you approve: your basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) and permission to upload videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts (video.upload).

How we use it
Your profile information is used only to show which account is connected. The upload permission is used only to send videos you created with the tool to your own TikTok drafts. Nothing is posted publicly by the app: you review and publish every draft yourself in the TikTok app.

Storage
Access tokens are stored only on the user's own computer. We do not run a server, do not collect analytics and do not store your data anywhere else.

Sharing
We do not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone.

Deleting your data
Disconnect the app in TikTok (Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions) and delete the tool's token file. The app then has no access and keeps no copy of your data.
